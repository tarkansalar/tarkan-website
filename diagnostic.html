The 3 Root Causes

There are only 3 root problems that break D2C brands at $3-15M. You have one.

ROOT CAUSE #1

Weak Positioning

The core issue: You don't own a clear niche in the market.

The Symptoms You're Treating:

High CAC Low conversion rates Price comparisons

Example:

Before: A skincare brand competing against 1,000 others. CAC: $85.

After: Repositioned as "skincare for women with hormonal acne". CAC dropped to $42. Conversion jumped to 3.8%.

ROOT CAUSE #2

No Clear Bestsellers

The core issue: You don't know which products to double down on.

The Symptoms You're Treating:

Overstock Thin margins Cash tied up

Example:

Before: Supplements brand with 25 SKUs. 28% margins. Cash tight.

After: Identified 4 hero products. Killed 15 underperformers. Margins jumped to 48%.

ROOT CAUSE #3

No Systems

The core issue: The business can't operate without you.

The Symptoms You're Treating:

Burnout Team underperformance Slow execution

Example:

Before: Founder working 75-hour weeks. Every decision went through her.

After: Built approval systems & playbooks. Hours dropped to 35/week. Revenue grew 40%.
